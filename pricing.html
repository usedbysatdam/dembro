Questions, answered

What is included in the free plan?

You get access to every free worksheet, game, lesson plan, and activity, plus personal collections and basic progress tracking. Premium resources are locked until you upgrade.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Memberships renew automatically, but you can cancel at any time from your dashboard and keep access until the end of the billing period.

Can I use Educrazy with my whole class?

Yes. Premium supports assignments for individual learners or a classroom, and school plans add rostering, admin reporting, and onboarding.

Do you offer homeschool discounts?

Homeschool families use the same premium membership. Annual billing brings the effective price down to about $10 per month.
